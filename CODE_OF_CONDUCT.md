# Code of conduct

Pokénux should be a welcoming place to learn, play, and contribute, regardless
of background, identity, or level of experience.

- Be respectful and patient. Assume good faith and ask for context.
- Critique ideas and code constructively; do not attack people.
- Respect people's boundaries, privacy, and time.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, publish private information, or post
  sexual content or unwanted sexual attention.

These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests, and other spaces
maintained for the project. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate
content, close discussions, or restrict participation when needed. Repeated
or serious violations may lead to a permanent ban.

Report a concern privately to **mrfrancoisbasset@gmail.com**. Include links or
context when possible. Reports will be handled with care; information will be
shared only as needed to investigate or address the situation. Maintainer
availability is voluntary, so an immediate response cannot be guaranteed.
